Profile

Gangil Yi is a sound and media artist working across installation, audiovisual performance, and interactive media. With sound as his primary medium, he treats technology and media themselves as artistic material, often through self-built hardware such as analog circuits and sound devices. As a member of Weather Report, a collective exploring live streaming as an artistic medium, he has created a wide range of sound-driven exhibitions, performances, and projects together with sound artist Ji-yeon Kim. Since 2019, he has been teaching sound synthesis and programming at a university of art and technology, and is currently a DFA candidate in media art.
